
# Functor rules {.dqlh}

A delightql functor (independent of usage -- i.e. table, function or semi-join) may be of three forms:

 - inchoate
 - argumentative
 - interior

## Inchoate {.dqlh}

An inchoate fuctor is the literal reference
to a ground relation GRELEX having nothing
internal to its parentheses.

```delighql
users()
```
